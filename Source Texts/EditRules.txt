RULES FOR CLEANING AND FORMATTING OCR JYOTISHA TEXTS

OBJECTIVE

Convert OCR-derived Jyotisha text into a clean, consistent and
retrieval-friendly corpus for NotebookLM.

The objective is textual restoration and structural formatting,
NOT reinterpretation or correction of the author's astrology.

The author's meaning, rules, qualifications, contradictions and
terminology must be preserved.


1. OCR CORRECTION

Correct obvious OCR errors in known English words.

Examples include:
- !, 1 or l incorrectly substituted for i
- 0, O and o incorrectly interchanged
- rn incorrectly read as m, or vice versa
- punctuation inserted inside words
- obvious OCR spelling corruption

Example:
    beneflc  -> benefic
    l!fe     -> life
    Mercu,:y -> Mercury

Do not alter a word merely because the author's original wording
appears unusual or old-fashioned.


2. WORD SPACING AND HYPHENATION

Restore spaces between words where OCR has joined them.

Example:
    ifyouare -> if you are
    Ifthe    -> If the

Join words incorrectly split across lines or pages.

Remove OCR-induced hyphenation where a word was broken only because
it occurred at the end of a printed line.

Preserve genuine compound-word hyphens.


3. CANONICAL PLANET NAMES

Use the following English names as the canonical planet names:

    Sun
    Moon
    Mercury
    Venus
    Mars
    Jupiter
    Saturn
    Rahu
    Ketu

Where Hindu/Sanskrit names occur, recognise them as equivalents:

    Ravi    = Sun
    Chandra = Moon
    Budh    = Mercury
    Shukra  = Venus
    Mangal  = Mars
    Kuja    = Mars
    Guru    = Jupiter
    Shani   = Saturn

Correct obvious OCR errors such as Buddha for Budh or Sani for Shani.

Prefer the canonical English names in structured rules because these
correspond to the terminology used in Parashar21 Chart.txt.

Do not replace Hindu names unnecessarily when they form part of the
author's original prose.


4. CANONICAL RASHI / SIGN NAMES

Use the following English names as the canonical sign names:

    Aries
    Taurus
    Gemini
    Cancer
    Leo
    Virgo
    Libra
    Scorpio
    Sagittarius
    Capricorn
    Aquarius
    Pisces

Correct OCR errors in these names.

Traditional Sanskrit/Hindu sign names may be retained where they are
part of the author's original discussion, but English sign names
should be preferred in structured rules so that terminology matches
Parashar21.


5. HOUSE NAMES

Replace references to numbered houses with consistent tokens:

    1st_house
    2nd_house
    3rd_house
    4th_house
    5th_house
    6th_house
    7th_house
    8th_house
    9th_house
    10th_house
    11th_house
    12th_house

Use these tokens in all structured rules.

Normal prose discussing a house in general may remain natural English
where appropriate.


6. HOUSE RULER NAMES

Normalize expressions such as:

    ruler of the 1st house
    lord of the 1st house
    1st house lord
    1st lord
    ruler of house 1

to:

    1st_ruler

Similarly use:

    2nd_ruler
    3rd_ruler
    ...
    12th_ruler

Where several rulers are mentioned, state each explicitly.

Example:

    rulers of the 1st, 3rd and 7th houses

becomes:

    1st_ruler, 3rd_ruler and 7th_ruler


7. PARAGRAPH AND DOCUMENT FORMATTING

Remove unnecessary blank lines, OCR artefacts, repeated headers,
footers and page-number debris.

Restore sensible paragraph boundaries.

Remove accidental duplicated text created during OCR.

Preserve meaningful headings and subheadings.

Do not rewrite the author's prose merely to improve literary style.


8. HOUSE ANALYSIS AND HOUSE-RULER RULES

For each house, retain the author's introductory description of the
house, including its general characteristics and karakas.

Then convert systematic house-ruler placement statements into
explicit IF-THEN rules.

Example:

Original structure:

    The ruler of the 12th house placed in the:

    1st HOUSE
    Poor health, thin or emaciated body...

    2nd HOUSE
    Financial losses...

Convert to:

    HR#1 IF 12th_ruler is in 1st_house THEN
    "Poor health, thin or emaciated body..."

    HR#2 IF 12th_ruler is in 2nd_house THEN
    "Financial losses..."

Continue for all available ruler/house combinations.

Number all House Rules sequentially:

    HR#1
    HR#2
    HR#3
    ...

Do not restart numbering for each section.


9. PLANETS IN HOUSES

Retain the introductory description of each house.

Convert systematic planet-in-house statements into explicit IF-THEN
rules.

Example:

Original:

    RAVI - THE SUN IN THE 12th HOUSE

    [general effects]

    UCHCHA & SWAKSHETRA
    [effects]

    NEECHA
    [effects]

Convert to:

    HR#501 IF Sun is in 12th_house THEN
    "[general effects]"

    HR#502 IF Sun is in 12th_house
    AND (Sun is exalted OR Sun is in own_sign)
    THEN "[effects]"

    HR#503 IF Sun is in 12th_house
    AND Sun is debilitated
    THEN "[effects]"

Use clear canonical condition terminology where possible:

    exalted
    debilitated
    own_sign
    retrograde
    combust

Preserve the author's original astrological meaning exactly.


10. DASHA RULES

Convert systematic Dasha statements into explicit IF-THEN rules
where the original text supports such a structure.

Number these separately and sequentially:

    D#1
    D#2
    D#3
    ...

Example:

    D#17 IF Jupiter is Mahadasha_lord AND [stated condition]
    THEN "[author's stated effects]"

Do not infer Dasha rules that the author has not explicitly stated.


11. SOURCE STRUCTURE AND PROVENANCE

Retain useful section headings so that the corpus remains readable
both to humans and to retrieval systems.

Example:

    ## HOUSE RULER PLACEMENTS

    ### 12th_ruler

    HR#397 IF 12th_ruler is in 1st_house THEN "..."
    HR#398 IF 12th_ruler is in 2nd_house THEN "..."

Where the original printed page number can be reliably identified,
retain it:

    [Source: JBR, p. 123]

Do not invent page numbers where provenance is uncertain.


12. UNCERTAIN OCR

Never reconstruct uncertain text using astrological knowledge.

If the OCR is too damaged to determine what the author actually wrote,
mark it:

    [UNCLEAR]

If only one word is uncertain:

    "...the planet produces [UNCLEAR] results..."

The objective is to preserve the source, not to guess what the author
probably intended.


13. PRESERVE THE AUTHOR'S ASTROLOGY

Do NOT:

- correct an astrological statement because it appears wrong
- add rules from general Jyotisha knowledge
- reconcile contradictory statements
- soften harsh predictions
- strengthen weak predictions
- modernize interpretations
- insert missing astrological conditions
- remove a statement merely because it appears implausible

If Braha says it, preserve it.

Correct the transmission, not the doctrine.


14. PRESERVE CONTRADICTIONS AND QUALIFICATIONS

Retain positive and negative statements, exceptions, qualifications
and apparent contradictions.

Do not attempt to harmonize them.

NotebookLM, not the editor, will later be asked to compare and
synthesize the evidence.


15. REMOVE IRRELEVANT OR CONTAMINATING MATERIAL

Material may be removed where it is not required for the Parashar21
analysis corpus, including:

- OCR-damaged computational tables already calculated by Parashar21
- redundant calculation instructions
- duplicated OCR passages
- irrelevant publishing/front/back matter
- worked celebrity horoscopes that could contaminate blind testing

Do NOT remove theoretical material merely because it appears unlikely
to be useful.


16. RETAIN RETRIEVAL-USEFUL TERMINOLOGY

Where possible, ensure that terminology used in the reference corpus
matches terminology generated by Parashar21.

In particular, prefer consistent forms for:

    planet names
    sign names
    house numbers
    house rulers
    dignity
    aspects
    conjunctions
    retrogression
    combustion
    Dasha
    Mahadasha
    Antardasha
    Yoga

Consistency is more important than stylistic variation in structured
rules.


17. DO NOT OVER-FORMAT

Not every sentence should become an IF-THEN rule.

Use structured rules where the source itself expresses a systematic
conditional relationship.

Retain explanatory, conceptual and interpretive passages as normal
prose.

The final corpus should therefore contain both:

    CLEAN EXPLANATORY PROSE

and

    EXPLICIT STRUCTURED RULES


18. FINAL PRINCIPLE

The cleaned corpus must remain a faithful representation of the
author's text.

The transformation is:

    OCR text
        -> repaired text
        -> normalized terminology
        -> explicit structure

It must NOT become:

    OCR text
        -> editor's interpretation
        -> rewritten Jyotisha text

Braha supplies the astrological knowledge.
The editor repairs and structures its transmission.
Parashar21 supplies the computed horoscope facts.
NotebookLM performs the interpretation.

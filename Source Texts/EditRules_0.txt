Rules for Editing and formatting OCR text

1. Fix spelling of known English words where letters like ! appear instead of i, 1, l and 0 or o,O has got exchanged

2. Introduce proper spaces between known English words where this is lost, "ifyouare" should he replaced with "if you are"

3. Make sure all correct names of planets and rashis are used, Sun, Moon, Mercury, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Rahu, Ketu and Aries, Taurus etc

4. If Hindu names are used, then Ravi, Chandra, Budh (not Buddha), Mangal ( avoid Kuja to the extent possible), Guru, Shani ( not Sani) and Mesh, Vrishhav etc (avoid Rashi names unless essential)

5. Replace text like 1st house, 2nd house etc with hyphenated words, 1st_house, 2nd_house .... 12th_house

6. Replace phrases like ruler of 1st house, 1st lord, 1st ruler etc with consistently one hyphenated word 1st_ruler, 2nd_ruler .... 12th_ruler. If you have to say rulers of 1st, 3rd or 7th, say instead 1st_ruler or 3rd_ruler or 7th_ruler

7. Remove extra lines and indent paragraphs as best as possible

RULES FOR CLEANING JYOTISHA OCR TEXT

OBJECTIVE

Create a clean, readable and retrieval-friendly text for use as an
LLM source.

Preserve the author's astrological meaning. Clean and organise the
text, but do not reinterpret, correct or add to the author's astrology.


1. CLEAN THE OCR INTELLIGENTLY

Correct obvious OCR errors, spelling errors, broken words, joined
words, missing spaces and punctuation.

Examples:

    Mercuiy       -> Mercury
    l!fe           -> life
    Ifthe          -> If the
    theperson      -> the person
    therfore       -> therefore

Use the meaning of the sentence to identify obvious OCR errors.

If the intended text cannot be determined confidently, use [UNCLEAR].
Do not guess from astrological knowledge.


2. USE CONSISTENT ASTROLOGICAL NAMES

Use these planet names in structured material:

    Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Rahu, Ketu

Recognise Hindu equivalents such as Ravi, Chandra, Budh, Mangal,
Guru, Shukra and Shani, but do not unnecessarily rewrite the
author's normal prose.

Use the standard English names of the Rashis:

    Aries, Taurus, Gemini, Cancer, Leo, Virgo, Libra, Scorpio,
    Sagittarius, Capricorn, Aquarius, Pisces


3. NORMALISE HOUSE REFERENCES

Whenever a number refers to a HOUSE, write:

    1st_house, 2nd_house ... 12th_house

This applies even when the word "house" is implied.

Examples:

    ninth house
        -> 9th_house

    Kendras are 1st, 4th, 7th and 10th
        -> Kendras are 1st_house, 4th_house, 7th_house and 10th_house

    Houses 1, 5 and 9 are dharma houses
        -> 1st_house, 5th_house and 9th_house are dharma houses


4. NORMALISE HOUSE RULERS

Whenever a number refers to the LORD or RULER of a house, write:

    1st_ruler, 2nd_ruler ... 12th_ruler

Examples:

    lord of the 6th house
        -> 6th_ruler

    rulers of the 2nd, 8th and 12th
        -> 2nd_ruler, 8th_ruler and 12th_ruler

Use the meaning of the sentence to distinguish a HOUSE from its RULER.


5. CREATE HOUSE RULES

Where the author gives systematic rules about house rulers or planets
occupying houses, convert them into IF-THEN statements.

Examples:

    HR#1 IF 12th_ruler is in 1st_house THEN "..."

    HR#2 IF Mars is in 4th_house THEN "..."

    HR#3 IF Sun is in 12th_house AND Sun is debilitated THEN "..."

Number all such rules sequentially:

    HR#1, HR#2, HR#3 ...


6. CREATE DASHA RULES

Where the author gives explicit rules about Dasha or Bhukti effects,
convert them into IF-THEN statements where appropriate.

Number them sequentially:

    D#1, D#2, D#3 ...


7. MAKE THE TEXT EASY TO READ

Do not produce a wall of text.

Keep blank lines between paragraphs and sections.

Use clear headings and subheadings.

Use indented bullets for lists.

Convert broken OCR lists into proper lists.

Join lines that were broken merely because of the printed page layout.


8. PRESERVE THE AUTHOR

Do not:

    • change an astrological rule because it appears wrong
    • add missing astrological knowledge
    • reconcile contradictions
    • soften or strengthen predictions
    • rewrite the author's ideas in your own words

Correct the TEXT, not the ASTROLOGY.


9. REMOVE CONTAMINATING EXAMPLES

Remove named celebrity horoscope examples that could reveal the
identity of blind-test cases.

Preserve the underlying general astrological rule whenever possible.


10. FINAL CHECK

Before finishing, read the cleaned text as an editor and proof-reader,  not as a search-and-replace program.

Check especially for:

    • remaining OCR nonsense
    • joined or broken words
    • malformed sentences
    • unnormalised house references
    • unnormalised ruler references
    • badly formatted lists
    • paragraphs broken by OCR line endings

The finished text should be both HUMAN-READABLE and LLM-READABLE.

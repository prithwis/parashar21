## PART 2: CLASSICAL COMBINATIONS

### 1. Gajakesari Yoga
*   **Horoscope Evidence:** Jupiter retrograde is placed in Taurus (Bhava 11), which is in the 7th house (a Kendra position) from the Moon in Scorpio (Bhava 5) [217-219].
*   **Textual Reference(s):** JBR 490, HM 349, YOG 1.
*   **Traditional Effect:** Confers generous nature, public popularity, financial prosperity, high administrative authority, and a lasting reputation [1, 349, 490].
*   **Modifying Factors:** The Moon is debilitated, but Jupiter's aspect on the Moon provides substantial protective energy [219, 490].
*   **Strength:** **Strong** (Jupiter has motional strength via retrogression) [61].

### 2. Damini (Damni) Yoga
*   **Horoscope Evidence:** All seven planets (excluding nodes) occupy exactly six signs of the zodiac: Aries, Taurus, Gemini, Virgo, Libra, and Scorpio [216-218].
*   **Textual Reference(s):** YOG 6.
*   **Traditional Effect:** Fosters lifetime wealth, philanthropic disposition, highly charitable nature, and a desire to help those in need [6].
*   **Modifying Factors:** Multiple planets are debilitated, which indicates that wealth is achieved through significant self-exertion and struggles rather than effortless luxury [95, 255].
*   **Strength:** **Strong**.

### 3. Dehakashta Yoga & Rogagrastha Yoga

## Part 2: Classical Combinations

### 1. Neechabhanga Rajayoga of Lagna Lord Mercury (Strong)
- **Horoscope Evidence**: Mercury (Lagna lord and 4th lord) is debilitated in Pisces (Bhava 10) [226] but is conjoined with Venus, which is exalted in Pisces [227, 228]. Additionally, the sign lord of Pisces, Jupiter, is placed in Aquarius [226], which is a Kendra (4th house) from the Moon in Scorpio [226].
- **Textual Reference(s)**: HM [255], JBR [474], BVR2 [134].
- **Traditional Effect**: Overcomes all failures, brings supreme intellectual success, unmatched academic/scientific honors, and makes the native an illustrious personality equal to a king in power and renown [11, 134, 255].
- **Modifying Factors**: Mercury is about 9.636° away from the Sun (Sun 11°20' Pisces, Mercury 10°58' Pisces) [225, 226]. Under BVR2 [55], it is within the 14° combustion range. However, under JBR [472], because Mercury has a higher longitude than the Sun and is more than 8° away, it is "past the point of combustion" and forms a brilliant **Buddhathi Yoga** for high intellectual capability [472].
- **Strength**: **Strong**. The cancelation is exceptionally robust because Venus is highly exalted in the same house [255, 474], and the sign lord Jupiter is angular from the Moon [134, 474].

### 2. Neechabhanga Rajayoga of Moon (Moderate)
- **Horoscope Evidence**: Moon (lord of Bhava 2) is debilitated in Scorpio in Bhava 6 [226]. However, Scorpio's sign lord Mars is highly exalted in Capricorn in Bhava 8 [226, 429].

- **Modifying Factors**: The Moon is in the 6th house (dusthana) [226], which indicates that the native must go through major disputes, health battles, or emotional hardships before realizing the fruits of this cancelation [159].
- **Strength**: **Moderate**. The debilitation lord (Mars) is highly exalted, but the Moon's placement in the 6th house slightly blemishes the physical comforts [159].

### 3. Malavya Yoga (Strong)
- **Horoscope Evidence**: Exalted Venus is located in Pisces in Bhava 10, which is a Kendra (quadrant) from Lagna [227, 228].
- **Textual Reference(s)**: HM [358], JBR [428, 475], BVR2 [112].

- **Modifying Factors**: Venus is conjoined with Saturn, Sun, and Mercury [228]. While the crowded stellium slightly blemishes the independent expression of Venus, the collective influence in the 10th house powerfully directs this energy toward supreme intellectual and aesthetic achievements [126].
- **Strength**: **Strong** (Venus consumes 24°49' Pisces, very close to its deep strength [227, 429]).

### 4. Gajakesari Yoga (Moderate)
- **Horoscope Evidence**: Jupiter is in Aquarius in Bhava 9, which is in the 4th house (a Kendra) from the Moon in Scorpio (Bhava 6) [226].
- **Textual Reference(s)**: BVR1 [187], JBR [473], HM [360].

- **Modifying Factors**: Moon is in Scorpio in the 6th house [226]. BVR1 [187] states: "If Gajakesari Yoga occurs in the 12th, 6th, or 8th from Lagna, it cannot advance the horoscopic indications for good." However, since Moon's debilitation is cancelled and Jupiter is in the highly auspicious 9th house, the yoga remains highly protective and spiritually enriching [187, 473].
- **Strength**: **Moderate**.

### 5. Vipareeta Raja Yoga of Mars (Strong)

- **Textual Reference(s)**: HM [258], BVR2 [125].

### 6. Buddhathi / Budha-Aditya Yoga (Moderate to Strong)

- **Strength**: **Moderate to Strong** (highly effective for academic distinction and scientific writing [105, 472]).

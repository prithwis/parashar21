## PART 2: CLASSICAL COMBINATIONS

### 1. Hamsa Yoga (Pancha Mahapurusha Yoga)
*   **Name:** Hamsa Yoga [130, 417].
*   **Horoscope Evidence:** Jupiter (Vrhaspathi) is situated in its own sign of Pisces (Meena) in Bhava 7 (a kendra from Lagna) [313].
*   **Textual References:** BVR2 (p. 284/285), HM (p. 417), JBR (p. 491).
*   **Traditional Effect:** The native is highly respected, virtuous, deeply learned in philosophies, blessed with a beautiful and faithful spouse, long-lived, and respected by the government and society [130, 417].
*   **Modifying Factors:** Jupiter is aspected by the Lagna itself and is unafflicted by any direct malefic conjunction, making this yoga remarkably pure and strong [315].
*   **Strength:** **Strong**.

### 2. Neechabhanga (Cancellation of Debility) for the Moon
*   **Name:** Neechabhanga / Neechabhanga Raja Yoga [158, 340].
*   **Horoscope Evidence:** The Moon is debilitated in Scorpio (Bhava 3) [313]. Its sign-lord, Mars (Mangal), is situated in Taurus (Bhava 9) [313]. Taurus is the 7th house (a kendra/quadrant) from the Moon's natal position [315]. Furthermore, Jupiter (which aspects Scorpio) and Venus (conjunct the Moon) provide powerful benefic support [315].
*   **Textual References:** BVR2 (pp. 17, 43, 158), HM (pp. 340, 434), JBR (p. 539).
*   **Traditional Effect:** When the lord of the sign where a planet is debilitated occupies a kendra (quadrant) from the Moon or Lagna, the debility is canceled, forming a Neechabhanga Raja Yoga [17, 158, 539]. This yields sudden rise, prosperity, and success after initial trials, replacing poverty with celebrity [156, 157].
*   **Modifying Factors:** Mars is retrograde, enhancing its strength, and Venus (lord of Taurus, the sign Mars occupies) is in a friendly association with the Moon, solidifying the cancellation [158, 171].
*   **Strength:** **Strong**.

### 3. Chandra-Mangala / Sasi-Mangala Yoga
*   **Name:** Sasi-Mangala / Chandra-Mangala Yoga [154, 527].
*   **Horoscope Evidence:** The Moon in Scorpio (Bhava 3) and Mars in Taurus (Bhava 9) are in mutual 7th-house aspect [315].
*   **Textual References:** BVR2 (p. 154), HM (p. 418, item iii), JBR (p. 541, item 41).
*   **Traditional Effect:** Generates immense energy, financial prosperity, courage, and organizing capability [331]. It ensures that the native earns through right and energetic means [7, 418].
*   **Modifying Factors:** Since the Moon is the 11th lord of gains and Mars is the lord of the 3rd and 8th, this yoga generates significant wealth through self-effort, though it can also make the native highly headstrong or prone to emotional impulsiveness [215, 517].
*   **Strength:** **Moderate to Strong**.

### 4. Dhana Yoga (Combination for Riches)
*   **Name:** Dhana Yoga [165, 170].
*   **Horoscope Evidence:** Conjunction of the 2nd and 9th lord Venus (Sukra) with the 11th lord Moon (Chandra) in Scorpio (Bhava 3) [314, 316], mutually aspected by the retrograde 3rd/8th lord Mars in Taurus (Bhava 9) [315].
*   **Textual References:** BVR2 (pp. 165, 170, 388), JBR (p. 542).
*   **Traditional Effect:** Gives immense wealth, landed properties, and success through documentation, creative writing, or artistic publishing [159, 166, 198]. 
*   **Modifying Factors:** Because this yoga occurs in the 3rd house of communication and self-effort, the wealth is not entirely inherited but is primarily self-acquired through the native's highly structured intellect and writing talents [509].
*   **Strength:** **Strong**.
